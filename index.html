Mixed-autonomy traffic pricing

Human drivers are selfish: they always take the fastest road. People using an autonomous-vehicle ride service pick their route by time and price, or decide to walk. Set the price of each route and watch how congestion across the network changes.

Based on Bıyık, Lazar, Pedarsani & Sadigh, “Incentivizing Efficient Equilibria in Traffic Networks with Mixed Autonomy,” IEEE Transactions on Control of Network Systems, 2021.
